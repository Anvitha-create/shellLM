# Ethical Considerations

shelLM is a research tool designed for cybersecurity education and honeypot research.

## Permitted Use
- Academic research on attacker behavior
- Cybersecurity education and training
- Authorized penetration testing environments
- Personal/lab environments you own

## Prohibited Use
- Deploying against systems without explicit written permission
- Collecting data on real users without consent
- Any illegal activity

## Responsible Deployment
- Only run on isolated, controlled environments
- Do not expose to the public internet without proper isolation
- Review logs ethically — treat any captured data responsibly
- Follow your institution's ethical guidelines for security research

Developed at Stratosphere Laboratory, Czech Technical University in Prague.
